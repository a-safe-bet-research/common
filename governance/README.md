# Governance documents

Ethics applications, decision letters, amendment supplements and data management plans for the A Safe Bet programme. Finalised 29 September 2026. Naming: `asb_ethics_<ref>_<version>[_decision|_supplement|_response].pdf`, `asb_dmp_<study>_vN.pdf`. Application and decision files are exports from the EUR ethics portal (DPECS Research Ethics Review Committee, ESSB); DMP files are DMPonline exports. Dates are those printed in the document. Files are grouped in `poc/` (the proof-of-concept study: three ethics applications with their decisions, supplements and the 2022 response, and the POC DMP) and `ebe_panel/` (the Experts by Experience panel study: application, decision and DMP); the review correspondence and this README sit at the folder root.
