# Ethics review correspondence: A Safe Bet applications

Record of the review exchanges for the programme's ethics applications, compiled 29 September 2026 from the EUR ethics portal (DPECS Research Ethics Review Committee, ESSB). The portal offers no export of review comments; this file transcribes the timeline, letters and comments as shown there. Reviewer comments are given in full where short and summarised where long; researcher replies are summarised. The application exports, supplements and decision letters are held alongside this file.

## ETH2122-0810: A Safe Bet POC, original application

Researchers: Harriet Galvin, Marilisa Boffo. Status: approved after amendments made.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 11 Jul 2022 | Application started |
| 16 Aug 2022 | Submitted |
| 13 Sep 2022 | Amendments requested (secretary: Tijs Gelens); formal notification sent |
| 10 Oct 2022 | Resubmitted |
| 12 Oct 2022 | Approved; decision letter sent. Valid three years |

Reviewer comments, 13 September 2022, and responses, 10 October 2022:

1. *Method.* The reviewer, having been told the intervention was a mandatory development under regulation, remained uneasy: "proof of concept" and the paragraph on tool efficacy suggested something new and experimental was being implemented and evaluated, with risks differing from treatment as usual. Response (summary): the operators implement the RG tools in line with regulatory requirements; POC denotes a small-scale version of the framework; "treatment as usual" was misleading terminology; splitting the player base into two groups would not comply with regulation; the tools would be implemented regardless of the study; players consent only to data sharing. The full response is held as `asb_ethics_eth2122_0810_v1_response`.
2. *Number of participants.* "Why this many? Is this the amount of players active on the gambling sites?" Response: yes, the estimated number of active gamblers for both operators.
3. *Informed consent.* "I wonder whether there is a possibility to include active consent?" Response (summary): consent covers data sharing only; the EUR data privacy officer confirmed that opt-out meets GDPR.
4. *Topic lists and questionnaires.* "I miss the survey questionnaires and some more content of the information the gamblers will receive." No reply comment; the interview schedule and user satisfaction surveys were added to the 10 October 2022 version.

## ETH2425-0364: A Safe Bet POC, amendment 2 (amends ETH2324-0128)

Status: approved after amendments made.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 12 Dec 2024 | Submitted |
| 13 Jan 2025 | Amendments requested (Tijs Gelens); formal notification sent |
| 16 Jan 2025 | Resubmitted; approved the same day. Valid three years |

Reviewer comments, 13 January 2025, on Amendment 3 (consent), and responses, 16 January 2025:

1. Is data sent to the University of Amsterdam also anonymised, or does no such transfer happen? Response: operators anonymise all data before transfer; no data goes to UvA, whose role is advisory.
2. With only an OKAY button, the notice must be impossible to miss ("This is ok I think, otherwise, research could not take place at all."). Response: a mandatory message at next login, linked to the privacy policies and the research page, with an OKAY button.
3. Can players ask customer service about the research? Response: customer service is briefed, has FAQs and can escalate to the EUR team.
4. How will players hear about the results? Response: results go on the operator research pages, OSF, newsletters and emails.
